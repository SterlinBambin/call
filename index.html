How this works

The call itself is peer-to-peer — your camera and mic connect directly to your guest's browser once they open the link, no separate call app needed.

Recording is local and separate from the call. When you hit Start recording, it captures your own camera/mic straight from your device at full quality (1080p, high bitrate) — not the compressed video going over the call. Your guest should hit their own Start recording button on their screen too, so you each end up with your own clean file. Send them to each other afterward the way you already do with separate camera/audio sources.

This page needs to be hosted somewhere with a real URL for the link to work for your guest — a static host like Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, or Vercel works, since there's no backend involved, just this one file.
